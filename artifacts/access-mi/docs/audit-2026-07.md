## 8. Positioning and content (owner decisions)

- IA duplication confuses users and crawlers: `/insights` vs `/data-and-insights` vs `/data` vs `/datasets` vs `/data-explorer`; `/civic-data` vs `/civic-data-hub`; `/partners` vs `/partnerships`; `/support` (donate) vs `/support-groups`.
- Positioning tension: the resident product is genuinely free/ad-free and the footer suppresses donation asks on help routes (good), but an enterprise BD funnel (`/bd-financial-model`, `/detection-gap`, `/market-intelligence`) and national-expansion lead capture live on the same domain. Worth a deliberate decision, possibly a separate partner subdomain or clearer separation.
- "Access Michigan Pro" paid tier is pre-announced on `/about` with no timeline.
- Terms effective date is Feb 2025 (~17 months); legal doc, owner should re-date after review.
- Changelog is the designated SSOT of release history but froze at April 2026 while Rounds 5-8 shipped (fixed in this PR through Round 8; keep it current).
- i18n adoption is ~6% of components; enabling ar/bn (A1) raises the value of extracting more strings, starting with the help-finding pages.
- Error tracking is absent (ErrorBoundary logs to console only); consider a privacy-respecting reporter consistent with the site's posture.
