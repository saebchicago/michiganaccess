# Access Michigan - 90-second walkthrough

Record this on the live site after the header/tour polish ships. Your voice. No AI voice. No music bed. 1080p. A $5 lavalier is enough.

## Setup

- Browser width about 1280px.
- Dismiss weather banners first.
- Use a ZIP people recognize: 48226 (Detroit), 48933 (Lansing), 49855 (Marquette), or 48322 (West Bloomfield).
- Have Find Help and one source document ready so you are not hunting on camera.

## Script (about 145 words)

**0:00-0:12 - name it**
"This is Access Michigan. Independent public data for all 83 counties. Not a government site. Every figure links to its source."

**0:12-0:32 - place**
Type the ZIP. Click Explore my area.
"Here is the snapshot for this ZIP. Health, money, and services measured against the state, with a label on the number."

**0:32-0:52 - one number**
Click one finding. Open the source.
"This dental shortage figure is modeled from HRSA. If I need the raw file, it is one click from here."

**0:52-1:12 - help**
Open Find Help. Search mental health or food in that ZIP.
"If someone needs help today, they do not have to read a dashboard first. Care, food, housing. No account."

**1:12-1:30 - close**
"Independent. Labeled. Built in Michigan. accessmi.org."

## 60-second county story variant

Pick one finding the data is sharp on: UP dental shortage, northern Michigan childcare slots, PFAS water bodies, or ALICE households.

1. Name the county.
2. Show the number and the label.
3. Show the source.
4. One sentence of implication. No policy speech.
5. End on the ZIP box.

## What not to do

- Do not generate B-roll of smiling families with laptops.
- Do not say "AI-powered" or "platform of record."
- Do not talk over a tour modal. If one appears, you are on an old build.
