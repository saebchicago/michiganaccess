# Access Michigan - one page for people who already talk to the public

**Site:** https://accessmi.org
**Short name:** AccessMI
**Public name:** Access Michigan
**What to call it in a sentence:** an independent public-data journal for Michigan's 83 counties.

## What it is

Access Michigan turns public records into civic intelligence so a resident can find help and an analyst can trace every number to its source.

It is not a government agency, not 211, not a health system, not a benefits administrator, and not ACCESS (the Arab Community Center for Economic and Social Services).

## What someone can do in two minutes

1. Enter a Michigan ZIP on the homepage.
2. Open Find Help for care, food, housing, or mental health. No account.
3. Click any featured number and read the source plus the VERIFIED / MODELED / PROJECTED label.

## What it is not for

- Enrolling anyone in a program. Applications stay on official portals.
- Crisis counseling. Use 988, text HOME to 741741, or 211. Those numbers are on every page.
- Treating Access Michigan figures as a substitute for a county health department or a hospital CHNA.
